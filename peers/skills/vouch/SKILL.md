---
name: vouch
description: >
  Check a claim before you believe it, act on it, or pass it on, and route a failed check back to its origin.
  "is this true", "check this claim", "verify before relaying", "can I trust this line", a claim about shared state or a session, a relayed ruling, or a finding about to enter the commons or go to a peer.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<check-the-claim>
Check a claim before you believe it, act on it, or pass it on; never pass on what you have not checked.

Check a claim about shared state or a session — a peer's, a relay's, your own belief about your model — against the thing itself; never take the claimant's word for it.
</check-the-claim>

<route-a-failed-check>
Tell the origin of a claim that fails your check, by the skill `tell`; never let a failed claim travel on.

Correct each failure of your own that reached a peer, by a `[notice]` to that peer through the skill `tell`; never hide one.
</route-a-failed-check>
