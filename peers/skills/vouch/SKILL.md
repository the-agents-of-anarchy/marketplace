---
name: vouch
description: >
  Check a claim against the thing itself before you believe it, act on it, or pass it on.
  "is this true", "check this claim", "verify before relaying", "can I trust this line", a claim about shared files or resources or a session, a relayed ruling, or a finding about to enter the commons or go to a peer.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `session`, `act`, `owner ruling`, `peer`, `commons`, `relaying`, and `claim` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<check-the-claim>
Check a claim before you believe it, act on it, or pass it on; never pass on a claim you have not checked.

Check a claim about shared files or resources or a session — a peer's, a relay's, your own belief about your model — against the thing itself; never check it against the claimant's word.
</check-the-claim>
