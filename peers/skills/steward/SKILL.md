---
name: steward
description: >
  Look after a shared thing for every session, in the open: name it on your card, take up an open stewardship, guard its canonical copy, and hand questions, resources, and the protocol to the skills that handle them.
  "steward this", "take up a stewardship", "release a stewardship", "who looks after X", "move a shared branch", a stewardship taken up, an open stewardship, or a copy diverging from a steward's.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `session`, `skill`, `member`, `protocol`, `card`, `[hazard]`, `[notice]`, `exclusive resource`, `stewardship`, `steward`, `open stewardship`, and `go-between` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<name-it-on-your-card>
Name each stewardship you keep in your card's `stewards`; never keep one off the card.

Take up a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never take one up silently.

Release a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never release one silently.

Keep at most one machine-wide stewardship; never take up a second.

Take up an open stewardship you can serve, by card edit and `[notice]`; never leave one open that you can serve.
</name-it-on-your-card>

<guard-the-canonical-copy>
Keep your stewardship's canonical copy, the one copy every session treats as true, on your own branch; never keep it elsewhere.

Flag by `[hazard]` a copy diverging from your canonical copy; never leave a divergence unflagged.

Reconcile a diverging copy on your own branch; never reconcile one on another member's branch.

Send a `[hazard]` before you force-move a shared branch, rewriting its history; never force-move one unwarned.

Send a `[notice]` after any move of a shared branch; never leave a move unannounced.
</guard-the-canonical-copy>

<pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
Take a questioned stewardship to a go-between by the skill `settle`; never decide the question alone.

Reserve an exclusive resource by the skill `reserve`; never keep one as a stewardship.

Serve the stewardship `protocol` by the skills `gather` and `ship`; never serve it by this skill alone.
</pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
