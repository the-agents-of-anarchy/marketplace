---
name: steward
description: >
  Look after a shared thing for every session, in the open: claim it on your card, take up an open stewardship, guard its canonical copy, and hand questions, resources, and the protocol to the skills that handle them.
  "steward this", "claim a stewardship", "release a stewardship", "who looks after X", "move a shared branch", a stewardship claim, an open stewardship, or a copy diverging from a steward's.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `stewardship`, `steward`, `open stewardship`, `card`, `session`, `peer`, `[hazard]`, `[notice]`, `go-between`, `exclusive resource`, and `scribe` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<name-it-on-your-card>
Name each stewardship you hold in your card's `stewards`; never hold one off the card.

Claim a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never claim one silently.

Release a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never release one silently.

Hold at most one machine-wide stewardship; never hold a second.

Take up an open stewardship you can serve, by card edit and `[notice]`; never leave one open that you can serve.
</name-it-on-your-card>

<guard-the-canonical-copy>
Keep your stewardship's canonical copy, the one copy every session treats as true, on your own branch; never keep it elsewhere.

Flag by `[hazard]` a copy diverging from your canonical copy; never leave a divergence unflagged.

Reconcile a diverging copy on a branch you hold; never reconcile one on a branch another member holds.

Send a `[hazard]` before you force-move a shared branch, rewriting its history; never force-move one unwarned.

Send a `[notice]` after any move of a shared branch; never leave a move unannounced.
</guard-the-canonical-copy>

<pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
Take a questioned stewardship to a go-between by the skill `settle`; never decide the question alone.

Hold an exclusive resource by the skill `reserve`; never hold one as a stewardship.

Serve the stewardship `protocol` by the skill `scribe`; never serve it by this skill alone.
</pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
