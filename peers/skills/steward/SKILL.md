---
name: steward
description: >
  Look after a shared thing for every session, in the open: claim it on your card, guard its canonical copy, take up what a departed member left, and hand questions, resources, and the protocol to the skills that settle them.
  "steward this", "claim a stewardship", "release a stewardship", "who looks after X", "move a shared branch", a stewardship claim, an open stewardship, or a copy diverging from a steward's.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<name-it-on-your-card>
Steward a shared thing by naming it in your card's `stewards`; never steward one off the card.

Claim a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never claim one silently.

Release a stewardship by card edit and `[notice]`; never release one silently.

Hold at most one machine-wide stewardship; never claim a second.

Take up an open stewardship a departed or gone member left, by card edit and `[notice]`, when you can serve it; never leave one open that you can serve.
</name-it-on-your-card>

<guard-the-canonical-copy>
Keep a steward's canonical copy on the steward's branch; never keep it elsewhere.

Flag a copy diverging from it elsewhere by `[hazard]` as the steward; never leave a divergence unflagged.

Reconcile a diverging copy as that branch's owner; never reconcile it as a third session.

Send a `[hazard]` before force-moving a shared branch; never force-move one unwarned.

Send a `[notice]` after any move of a shared branch; never move one unannounced.
</guard-the-canonical-copy>

<pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
Take a questioned stewardship to a go-between by the skill `settle`; never settle it by who claimed first.

Hold an exclusive resource by the skill `reserve`; never hold one as a stewardship.

Serve the stewardship `protocol` by the skill `scribe`; never serve it by this skill alone.
</pass-on-what-other-skills-handle>
