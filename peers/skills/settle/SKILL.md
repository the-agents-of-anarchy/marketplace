---
name: settle
description: >
  Take a contested matter to a go-between, divide what can exist twice when mediation fails, and bring the owner what neither settles.
  "settle a dispute", "we disagree", "two sessions want this", "find a go-between", a dispute between sessions, a questioned stewardship, or a contested exclusive resource.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `session`, `card`, `owner`, `bringing a matter to the owner`, `brought matter`, `[ask]`, `peer`, `exclusive resource`, `stewardship`, and `go-between` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<ask-a-go-between>
Settle among peers what peers can settle; never spend the owner's attention on it.

Ask a go-between to mediate a contested matter, by the skill `tell`; never mediate a matter you are party to.

Weigh a go-between's proposal as advice; never weigh it as binding.
</ask-a-go-between>

<divide-what-can-exist-twice>
Divide what can exist twice when mediation fails — fork it, split it, or keep two copies; never force one side onto the other.
</divide-what-can-exist-twice>

<bring-the-owner-the-rest>
Bring a failed mediation over an exclusive resource to the owner; never settle it yourself.

Bring any other dispute to the owner when both sides ask; never bring it when one side asks.

Keep both copies of a brought dispute standing apart while it waits; never force one meanwhile.
</bring-the-owner-the-rest>

<note-the-outcome>
Note a settled stewardship on the cards by the skill `steward`; never leave its outcome off them.
</note-the-outcome>
