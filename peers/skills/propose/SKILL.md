name: propose
description: >
  Bring a protocol change to the scribe as a draft's source: report a defect or propose a change, in a file, and hold the request open through its round.
  "propose a protocol change", "change the peer protocol", "report a protocol defect", or a defect met in the protocol text.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `scribe`, `protocol change`, `draft`, `round`, `journal`, `stamp`, `checkout`, and `[notice]` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<report-a-defect>
Send the scribe `[notice] protocol: <defect>` for each defect you meet in the protocol text; never leave a defect unreported.
</report-a-defect>

<propose-a-change>
Send the scribe `[ask] protocol: <change>` to propose a change; never send it under a type of your own.

Put the problem, its evidence as a journal path with the exact stamp, and the wording in a file in your checkout; never send a summary in place of the file.

Inline that file's content when it fits in ten lines; never inline more than ten lines.

Hold a request open until the scribe reports its outcome; never close it on silence.

Follow your request's round by the skill `protocol-change`; never follow it another way.
</propose-a-change>
