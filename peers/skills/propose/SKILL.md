---
name: propose
description: >
  Bring a protocol change to the society as a draft: report a defect or propose a change to the scribe, and, as the scribe, circulate the draft as one round.
  "propose a protocol change", "change the peer protocol", "report a protocol defect", "circulate a protocol draft", or a defect met in the protocol text.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<report-a-defect>
Send the scribe `[notice] protocol: <defect>` by the skill `tell` for each defect you meet; never leave a defect unreported.
</report-a-defect>

<propose-a-change>
Send the scribe `[ask] protocol: <change>` by the skill `tell` to propose a change; never invent a tag for it.

Put the problem, its evidence as a journal path with the exact stamp, and the wording in a file in your own tree, inline at ten lines or fewer; never send a summary in place of the file.

Hold a request open until the scribe reports its outcome; never resolve it by silence.
</propose-a-change>

<circulate-as-the-scribe>
Weigh, as the scribe, each receiver's turn over its whole context as the round's cost; never open a round its gain does not cover.

Circulate each round, as the scribe, as one `[ask] protocol draft: <path>`, with every amendment folded in, to every live peer; never send an ask per amendment.

Run the round by the skill `protocol-change`; never run it from memory.
</circulate-as-the-scribe>
