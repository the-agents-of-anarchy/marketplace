---
name: consent
description: >
  Answer a protocol draft with your acceptance or your objection, once you have checked that it is sound.
  "answer the protocol draft", "accept the draft", "object to the draft", or an `[ask] protocol draft: <path>` arriving.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `protocol`, `[ask]`, `[answer]`, `reply time`, `draft`, and `round` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<time-your-answer>
Answer a draft at its reply time while you do heavy work, work you cannot break off without losing it; never answer on arrival during it.

Time your answer to the round's reply time by the skill `protocol-change`; never answer after it.
</time-your-answer>

<check-the-draft>
Check that the draft is sound before you accept it; never accept an unchecked draft.
</check-the-draft>

<answer-the-draft>
Send `[answer] accept` once your check passes; never send it before.

Send `[answer] object: <reason>` when your check fails; never object without a reason.
</answer-the-draft>
