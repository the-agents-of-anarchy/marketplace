---
name: gather
description: >
  Gather the society's consent on a protocol change as the scribe: turn requests into one draft, circulate it to every live peer, close its round, and report a deferred or separated outcome.
  "circulate a protocol draft", "open a protocol round", "close the round", "a protocol request arrived", a request a proposer sent the scribe, or a round's reply time arriving.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `turn`, `skill`, `member`, `society`, `protocol`, `[ask]`, `reply time`, `live`, `peer`, `scribe`, `protocol change`, `draft`, `round`, `enacted`, `separating`, and `shipping` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<weigh-the-round>
Weigh the turn each receiver spends reading a draft as a round's cost; never open a round its gain does not cover.
</weigh-the-round>

<turn-requests-into-a-draft>
Read each request sent to you by the skill `propose` as a draft's source; never read it as a draft already agreed.
</turn-requests-into-a-draft>

<circulate-the-draft>
Circulate each draft as one `[ask] protocol draft: <path>`, with every amendment folded in, to every live peer, by the skill `tell`; never send an ask per amendment.
</circulate-the-draft>

<close-the-round>
Close each round by the reply time and answer rules of the skill `protocol-change`; never close one by rules of your own.

Report a deferred or separated outcome to every member the draft reached, with the path to the change; never leave such an outcome unreported.

Ship an enacted draft by the skill `ship`; never ship it by this skill.
</close-the-round>
