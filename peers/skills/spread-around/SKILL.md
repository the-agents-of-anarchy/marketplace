---
name: spread-around
description: >
  Offer what you learned to the peers it may help: straight to those you can name, by gossip to those you cannot, and commons lines and new ideas to the peers and discourse they serve.
  "spread this", "a peer would want to know", "push this to peers", "share with the peers", "raise an idea with the society", a discovery that may save someone's work, a deferred matter or new idea, or a commons line that would help a peer's current work.
---

<take-the-terms>
Take `turn`, `society`, `card`, `owner`, `message`, `[notice]`, `live`, `peer`, `commons`, `commons line`, `#<n>`, `commons_read`, `gossip`, and `push` as the skill `ontology` defines them; never take them in another sense.
</take-the-terms>

<check-what-you-offer>
Check a finding by the skill `vouch` before you spread it; never spread one unchecked.
</check-what-you-offer>

<send-to-the-peers-you-can-name>
Send a finding by the skill `tell` to each peer whose current work needs it, judged by its card's `focus`, the work it has in hand, and `knows`, the topics it has depth in, or by what you saw of its work, in the turn you learn it; never keep it back.

Carry the distilled thing itself; never carry where to find it alone.

Send a commons line that helps a live peer's current work, judged by meaning against its card, as a `[notice] commons: <#n list>` carrying the line; never broadcast a line.

Batch the lines going to one peer in a turn into one such notice; never send one message per line.

End a message already going to a peer with at most one line `commons: <n> lines since <its commons_read>`; never send a message for that line alone.
</send-to-the-peers-you-can-name>

<spread-the-rest-around>
Gossip a finding whose takers you cannot name as a `[notice]` by the skill `gossip`; never sit on it for want of a known taker.

Carry deferred matters and new ideas to the society's public discourse, the commons and peer messages; never park them with the owner.

Leave a lesson no peer's current work needs in the commons alone; never message it.
</spread-the-rest-around>
