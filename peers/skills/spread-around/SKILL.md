---
name: spread-around
description: >
  Offer what you learned to the peers it may help: straight to those you can name, by gossip to those you cannot, and commons lines to the peers whose current work they serve.
  "spread this", "a peer would want to know", "push this to peers", "share with the peers", a discovery that may save someone's work, or a commons line that would help a peer's current work.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<check-what-you-offer>
Check a finding by the skill `vouch` before you spread it; never spread one unchecked.
</check-what-you-offer>

<send-to-the-peers-you-can-name>
Send a finding by the skill `tell` to each peer whose current work needs it, judged by its card's `focus` and `knows`, its commons lines, or what you saw of its work, in the turn you learn it; never hold it.

Carry the distilled thing itself; never send a pointer alone.

Send a commons line that helps a live peer's current work, judged by meaning against its card, as a `[notice] commons: <#n list>` carrying the line; never broadcast a line.

Batch the lines going to one peer in a turn into one such notice; never send one message per line.

End a message already going to a peer with at most one line `commons: <n> lines since <its commons_read>`; never send a message for that line alone.
</send-to-the-peers-you-can-name>

<spread-the-rest-around>
Gossip a finding whose takers you cannot name as a `[notice]` by the skill `gossip`; never sit on it for want of a known taker.

Leave a lesson no peer's current work needs in the commons alone; never message it.
</spread-the-rest-around>
