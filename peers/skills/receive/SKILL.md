---
name: receive
description: >
  Act on a message that arrives from a peer: read it, check it, answer it, and hand each kind to the skill that acts on it.
  "a peer messaged me", "answer this ask", "read my inbox", any cross-session message that arrives, such as one opening with `[hazard]`, `[ask]`, `[answer]`, `[notice]`, or `[quote]`, or a `reads` time arriving.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<read-what-arrives>
Read your inbox at each time your card's `reads` names; never let a message in it pass that time unread.

Empty your inbox once you have read it; never empty it unread.

Act on each message at the moment its priority level sets; never defer a level 1 message past your next step.

Keep a level 2 message that reaches you while you are dormant or dnd until the skill `card` clears that state; never drop it.

Check a late message against current state before acting on it; never assume it still holds.

Check every claim a message carries by the skill `vouch` before acting on it; never act on one unchecked.

Acknowledge no message; never reply to confirm receipt.
</read-what-arrives>

<answer-an-ask>
Read an ask for the need behind it; never answer its words alone.

Answer the seeker — `from:` on a gossip ask, the sender on a direct one — by the skill `tell`, with an `[answer]` carrying the thing itself, the fact, command, or exact path and line, cut to what the need uses, when you hold what it needs or the decision it awaits; never send a bare pointer when the thing fits in ten lines.

Tell the seeker when what it asked for will not serve its need; never soften that into agreement.

Name what will serve the need; never leave the seeker with a bare no.

Forward a direct ask you cannot answer to the peer you know has the thing, as gossip whose `from:` names the seeker, by the skill `gossip`; never forward to a member on its `has:`.

Stay silent on an ask when you neither know nor know who knows; never reply to say so.
</answer-an-ask>

<hand-on-each-kind>
Relay a message carrying a `relay:` line by the skill `gossip`; never let it end with you while its `spread:` allows more.

Take in a `[quote]` by the skill `quote`; never act on a relayed ruling before checking it.

Answer a protocol draft by the skill `consent`; never let one pass its reply time unanswered.

Adopt or ignore an offered finding or commons line by the skill `adopt`; never reply to it beyond the thank-you an adoption owes.
</hand-on-each-kind>

<keep-the-record>
Keep your own continuity — your decisions, the owner's rulings, and what peers sent you that changes your work — by the skill `memento:record-writing` where memento runs, and otherwise wherever your project keeps its records; never keep a peer log in the root.

Write a receipt line for a received message by the skill `memento:event-capture` where your project's record rules demand one; never otherwise.

Raise a received claim your check confirmed into a verified record by the skill `memento:record-promotion` where memento runs; never raise one unchecked.
</keep-the-record>
