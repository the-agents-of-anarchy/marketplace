---
name: gossip
description: >
  Pass a message from peer to peer past anyone its origin could name, inside the envelope that lets whoever holds or needs it reach the origin directly.
  "gossip this", "pass it on", "relay this", a need or finding you cannot target to a known peer, or a gossip message arriving with a `relay:` line.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<gossip-what-you-cannot-target>
Send a need or a finding straight to the peer you know holds or needs it, by the skill `tell`; never gossip what you can target.

Originate a gossip `[ask]` by the skill `ask-around` and a gossip `[notice]` by the skill `spread-around`; never originate either by this skill alone.

Gossip often, in any project; never save gossip for emergencies.
</gossip-what-you-cannot-target>

<build-the-envelope>
Open the body of every gossip with the envelope, its lines in the order `from:`, `spread:`, `relay:`, `has:`; never send gossip without it.

Write `from: <member id> <session name>` naming the origin, you or the seeker whose ask you forward; never name a relayer there.

Write `spread: <free text>` as the origin, naming the scope, fan-out, or stop you choose — hops, count, time, or until answered; never set a constraint you did not choose.

Write the fixed line `relay: pass this on as spread: says, adding yourself to has: first; if you hold what it seeks or it changes your work, reach from: directly.`; never alter it.

Write `has: <members>`, the members that already hold the gossip — your own member id and each recipient's; never list a member that does not hold it.

Count the envelope within the ten-line body, moving content that does not fit to a file in your own tree named by its path; never exceed ten lines.

Send the gossip as a message by the skill `tell`; never invent a type for it.
</build-the-envelope>

<relay-as-the-spread-says>
Relay a gossip on seeing its `relay:` line, whatever skill text you hold; never let it end with you while its `spread:` allows more.

Read a gossip with no `spread:` line, or one naming no scope, as asking each receiver to pass it to at least one member not on `has:` and not barred by status; never drop it for want of a scope.

Pick the recipients by any means, your own judgment of fit included or left out; never add a constraint of your own to the origin's.

Add yourself to `has:` before you pass a gossip on, and each recipient as you send; never add a member that does not hold it.

Skip each member on `has:` when you relay; never pass a gossip to a member already on it.

Hold a relay of a level 2 gossip for a member it would reach while busy, dormant, or dnd, as the skill `tell` holds a level 2 message; never relay to that member before the state clears.

Read `has:` as local to the path its copy travelled; never take a second copy of one gossip for a new gossip.

Relay and answer a gossip once, however many copies reach you; never relay or answer a copy of one you already hold.

Keep `from:`, `spread:`, and `relay:` as the origin wrote them; never change them.

Correct a checkable error in the content before relaying, marking it; never relay a known error.

Drop a gossip current state has outdated; never relay it.
</relay-as-the-spread-says>

<reach-the-origin>
Reach `from:` directly by the skill `tell` when you hold what the gossip seeks or it changes your work, with an `[answer]` carrying the thing itself or the thank-you an adoption owes; never reply through the chain.
</reach-the-origin>
