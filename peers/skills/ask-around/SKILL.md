---
name: ask-around
description: >
  Seek what you need from peers you cannot name, by gossip, after your own cheap effort and a direct ask to any peer you know holds it.
  "ask around", "who knows", "find someone who has", "pull from peers", or a need your own records, the commons, and a search do not meet.
---

<read-the-terms>
Read every society term here as the skill `ontology` defines it; never read one in another sense.
</read-the-terms>

<spend-your-own-effort-first>
Spend your own cheap effort first — your own records, the commons, the docs, a search; never ask a peer what minutes of your own work answer.
</spend-your-own-effort-first>

<ask-whom-you-can-name>
Send a direct `[ask]` by the skill `tell` to the peer you know holds the thing; never gossip a need you can target.
</ask-whom-you-can-name>

<ask-around-for-the-rest>
Gossip an `[ask]` by the skill `gossip` when you know no peer who holds the thing; never stop at the commons.

Carry in every ask the need behind the question, what you tried, and what would end your search; never send a bare question.
</ask-around-for-the-rest>

<close-the-search>
Stop asking once an answer meets the need; never ask on.

Read a peer's silence on your ask as "doesn't know"; never chase it.

Tell a peer in one `[notice]` line when its answer missed the need; never let a bad answer stand.
</close-the-search>
